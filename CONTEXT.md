# Biblical Hebrew learning

This context describes the language-learning concepts used by the Althebräisch trainer, from reading Hebrew forms to understanding words and short biblical passages.

## Language

**Lexeme**:
A dictionary entry for a word and its core meaning. A verb lexeme is shown with a usable citation form as well as its consonantal root when known.
_Avoid_: Vocabulary form, root (as a synonym)

**Root**:
The consonantal base that connects related Hebrew lexemes and forms. A root is not itself necessarily a word a learner can use or translate.
_Avoid_: Lemma, dictionary form

**Word form**:
A specific vocalized Hebrew form that occurs or is taught in context, connected to its lexeme and any relevant grammatical features.
_Avoid_: Root, vocabulary item

**Grammar rule**:
A reusable pattern that helps identify how a word form functions or changes in a phrase.
_Avoid_: Translation hint (as a synonym)

**Verse exercise**:
A short biblical phrase or verse that links its Hebrew word forms to lexemes, grammar rules, and a contextual translation.
_Avoid_: Unlinked translation prompt
